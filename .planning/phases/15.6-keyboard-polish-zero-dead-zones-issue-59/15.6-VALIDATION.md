## Manual-Only Verifications

| Behavior | Requirement | Why Manual | Test Instructions |
|----------|-------------|------------|-------------------|
| Zero dead zones | Dead zones eliminated | Touch routing is visual/haptic — requires real device tap testing | Tap every gap between keys, every row boundary, every edge. Must get haptic + letter for every tap. |
| Edge key feedback | Edge touch same as center | Haptic timing (touchDown vs touchUp) requires real device | Tap left edge of A, right edge of P, bottom of space bar. Haptic must fire on touchDown, popup must appear. |
| Popup visual polish | Larger letter, connected stem | Visual quality assessment | Compare popup appearance to Apple keyboard. Letter should be visually larger, stem should connect seamlessly to key. |
| Gesture preservation | Accent, delete, space trackpad, shift | Multi-touch gesture timing requires real device | Long-press E for accents, hold delete for repeat acceleration, swipe space bar, double-tap shift for caps lock. |
